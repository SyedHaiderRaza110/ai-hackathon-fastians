# FASTIANS — Executive Summary

## What It Is

FASTIANS is a trusted student exchange platform built for FAST students. It replaces the fragmented way students currently discover and coordinate resources — scattered WhatsApp groups, physical noticeboards, and one-off messages — with a single, organized exchange layer for the university community.

**Tagline:** Everything FAST students share, in one place.

## The Problem

Students routinely use WhatsApp groups, noticeboards, and individual messages to sell or find textbooks and equipment, find tutors, offer peer services, coordinate shared resources, and check slot availability. This leads to:

- Listings buried in chat history
- No easy way to browse what's available right now
- Manual, one-on-one coordination for everything
- Unclear availability and double-booking
- Little structured trust between students

**Problem statement:** Students need a fast, trustworthy way to discover, exchange, and book resources within their own university community without digging through scattered group chats.

## The Solution

FASTIANS is **not** a marketplace clone (not another OLX). It is a community exchange layer that lets students:

- Find academic resources (textbooks, calculators, notes, electronics, equipment)
- Buy or sell useful items
- Offer or discover peer tutoring
- Offer or find student-to-student services (CV review, design, programming help)
- Book peer-provided services or resources with conflict-free scheduling
- Contact other students through controlled, lightweight requests
- Build trust through completed exchanges and reviews

**Guiding principle: Community first, marketplace second.** The product surfaces things a generic marketplace never would — a senior offering DSA tutoring, a peer reviewing a CV, a mentor with two open slots tomorrow.

## Why It's Different

| Generic Marketplace (OLX-style) | FASTIANS |
|---|---|
| Buy/sell only | Resources + tutoring + services + bookings |
| No campus context | University-specific, community-first |
| No structured trust | Ratings + completed-exchange history |
| No scheduling | Conflict-free slot booking |
| Anyone, anywhere | FAST students only |

## Core Experience

A student can discover a listing, view the owner's trust profile, request contact or book a slot, coordinate and complete the exchange, and leave a review — the same loop for a textbook sale, a tutoring session, or a CV review.

```text
Discover → View → Request Contact / Book → Connect → Exchange → Complete → Review → Trust
```

## Scope Discipline

To stay buildable in a 24-hour hackathon window, FASTIANS deliberately excludes: a full e-commerce platform, a social network, a university ERP, a full chat application, a payment platform, a complex room-management system, an AI chatbot, and any large feature collection. A smaller feature set that works flawlessly beats a large one that breaks.

## Why It Wins

The strongest story is real student pain, a simple solution, and a complete end-to-end workflow — not technical novelty. The goal is for a judge to leave thinking **"That actually solves a problem we have,"** not "they used a cool AI model."

**What "done" looks like:** a listing created in under a minute, a useful search result in seconds, a slot booked in a few clicks, a visible trust history, and a clear path from discovery to review.
